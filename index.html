Your 4-day gym plan, Georgie

Georgie's Workouts

A beginner glute-focused program for a full gym. Four 60-minute sessions a week, almost all lifting: two lower-body days, one upper-body day and one full-body day. Every move has a demo video and a "How to do it" section under it.

Any days work. Just try not to put the two lower-body days (Day 1 and Day 3) back to back. On rest days, an easy 30–45 minute walk is great for recovery.

How to get stronger each week

This plan uses double progression, the same method behind most popular glute programs.

  • Pick a weight you could lift for 2–3 more reps at the end of each set. The last reps should feel hard but your form should stay clean.
  • Each session, try to add a rep or two.
  • Once you hit the top rep number on every set (for example 3×12), add weight next time: about 5–10 lb for hip thrusts, leg press and deadlifts, or 2.5–5 lb for arms and shoulders. Then build the reps back up.
  • Weeks 1–2: keep it light and focus on learning the moves. Watch each video before your first set.
  • Every 8 weeks: take an easy week at about half the sets, then keep going.

Write your weights in the box on each exercise. It saves on this phone so you can beat it next time.

Why it's built this way
  • Glutes twice a week. Hitting a muscle about twice a week with enough hard sets tends to build it better than one big day.
  • Hip thrusts, hinges, squats and lunges. These three movement types work the glutes from different angles, which is why they show up in nearly every well-known women's glute plan.
  • Upper body too. Back and shoulder work gives a toned, balanced shape and better posture.
  • Machines and dumbbells first. They're easier to learn than barbell lifts and let beginners train hard safely.
  • Almost all lifting. Each hour is a 5-minute warm-up, then machines, cables and dumbbells. Stretching afterward is optional.
  • Built from the moves the big women's programs share. Hip thrusts, RDLs, hack squats, Bulgarian split squats, cable kickbacks and the abduction machine show up again and again, at 3–4 sets of 8–15 reps.
